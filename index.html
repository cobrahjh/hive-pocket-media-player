    Sound

    Microphone

    Not listening.

    The one source that can see sound this app does not own — another app's music, a speaker in the room, a stream. Nothing is recorded and nothing leaves the phone; the sound is measured and drawn, never saved and never sent.

    While it is listening the screen is kept awake, so a phone propped against something does not go dark mid-track. Measured cost: about 12% of a battery an hour, screen and all.

    App

    On Automatic the app starts from what this phone says about itself, then watches its own frame rate while it draws and moves between the three settings below. It steps down within about two seconds of stuttering and back up only after several seconds of running clean, so it settles rather than flickering between two. The line above always says which one is actually running — Automatic never means "Full, probably".

    Bursts throw fewer pieces and the background carries fewer as this comes down, and Battery saver also redraws the equalizer 24 times a second instead of 60 — visibly less fluid on a fast track. Nothing is removed and no effect becomes unavailable. Full costs about 12% of a battery an hour with the microphone on; what the other two save against that has not been measured.

    Player
    Your music folder

    No folder remembered yet.

    This browser can remember a folder, so your music comes back without picking it again. It cannot reopen it silently — a page is never allowed to reach your files unasked — so when the browser wants permission again you get one tap rather than the whole picker. It reads up to 500 tracks and three folders deep, and says so when a folder is bigger than that.

    To stop being asked at all: when Chrome shows the permission prompt it offers three answers, and the middle one, Allow on every visit, is the one that sticks. The default only covers this visit, which is why it asks again.

    Installing this app to your home screen makes that permanent — Chrome keeps folder permission for an installed app without asking again. It also opens without the browser bars, which leaves more room for the visuals.

    How it works
    • Microphone — in this menu. It draws from whatever the phone can hear, which is the only way to get visuals from music this app cannot read — YouTube, Spotify, another phone across the room, a radio. Play it out loud and the bars and bursts follow it.
    • Folder button — choose music from this phone. On Chrome the app remembers the folder and brings it back with one tap next time; on other browsers it asks again each time, because nothing there can remember one.
    • Link button — paste a direct address to an audio file. Saved links come back every time.
    • Tap the visuals for full screen. Tap again to come back.
    • Drag a finger across them and they paint — bursts follow your finger, in whatever effect and colours are set. A press and hold does it too. It works with no music at all, which makes it the one thing here that needs nothing but a finger.
    • Star button — effects on or off. It is the same setting as On the stage above.
    • Previous goes back a track, or starts the current one again if you are more than a few seconds in.
    • YouTube was removed in 1.18.0. Its sound came from another site, which a web app is not allowed to read, so it was the one thing here that played to a dead stage — no bars, no bursts. Play it out loud in the YouTube app and press Listen instead, and the visuals follow it properly. Links saved before are kept but say so when pressed.
    • Reacts to — which parts of the sound throw a burst. Bass is the kick drum; mids are voices and guitars, mid-screen; highs are hats and cymbals, near the top.
    • Lightning is in both the Bursts list and the finger setting. A bolt strikes on the beat, or down to wherever you touch. It flashes, so Random and Surprise me never choose it for you.
    • Report a problem — in this menu. Write what happened and press Send by email; your mail app opens with the report and the app's own state already in it, and you press send there. Nothing about your music is included, and you can read exactly what is attached before you send it.
    • Everything works offline now, including the next time you open the app. Nothing in this app reaches the network at all.
    About

    Hive Pocket Media Player
    Everything runs on this phone and your music is never uploaded. Since 1.18.0 nothing here reaches the network at all — YouTube was the last thing that did. Reporting a problem does not change that: the app hands the text to your mail app, which is the thing that sends it, after you press send in it.

    On a browser that allows it — Chrome on Android since early 2025, and Chromium on the desktop — the app remembers your music folder and offers it back with one tap. It can never reopen it silently; no page is allowed to reach your files unasked. Anywhere else it asks for your music each time, because nothing there can remember a folder and copying every track into storage would double the space and could be wiped without warning. Saved links come back everywhere.
