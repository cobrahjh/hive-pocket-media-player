    Sound

    Microphone

    Not listening.

    The one source that can see sound this app does not own — another app's music, a speaker in the room, a stream. Nothing is recorded and nothing leaves the phone; the sound is measured and drawn, never saved and never sent.

    While it is listening the screen is kept awake, so a phone propped against something does not go dark mid-track. Measured cost: about 12% of a battery an hour, screen and all.

    App

    How it works
    • Microphone — in this menu. It draws from whatever the phone can hear, which is the only way to get visuals from music this app cannot read, YouTube included. Play it out loud and the bars and bursts follow it.
    • Folder button — choose music from this phone. It asks every time you open the app fresh, because a browser cannot remember a folder between sessions.
    • Link button — paste an audio address, or a YouTube video or playlist. Saved links come back every time.
    • Tap the visuals for full screen. Tap again to come back.
    • Star button — effects on or off. It is the same setting as On the stage above.
    • Previous goes back a track, or starts the current one again if you are more than a few seconds in.
    • YouTube plays in its own player, so it needs a connection. It gets no equalizer or effects: that sound comes from another website and this app cannot see it.
    • Everything else works offline, including the next time you open the app.
    About

    Hive Pocket Media Player
    Everything runs on this phone and your music is never uploaded. The only thing that reaches out is YouTube, and only while a YouTube track is playing.

    It asks for your music each time it starts cold: a phone will not let a web app remember a folder, and copying every track into storage would double the space and could be wiped without warning. Saved links do come back.
